Short description

Steadylapse puts a whole timelapse studio in one Android app. Clouds, city lights, a drawing, a garden or a study session: set the interval, turn the screen off and walk away — it keeps shooting for hours and builds the video automatically. Eight Color Effects can be previewed before the shoot and changed afterwards, re-rendering any shoot without touching the original. Manual ISO, shutter and white balance, every lens, RAW (DNG) frames for Lightroom, saved setups, Ghost Frame for multi-day projects and a beta "Holy Grail" exposure ramp. No laptop, no extra hardware, no ads, no account.
